About

The visual world of aquascaping

Curated by the people behind Hakkai and Pet Zone Tropical Fish.

The Scape Gallery exists to do one job: inspire. It's a gallery of finished aquascapes, a portfolio of layouts and installations, and a source of visual reference for anyone trying to picture what a "living landscape" actually looks like before they build one — or before they even know they want to.

It deliberately isn't a store, and it isn't a how-to guide. Those already exist, and they're good at what they do:

SiteMain job
The Scape GalleryInspire, curate, showcase, tell the story behind each piece
HakkaiTeach the craft and develop the aquascaper — workshops, demos, hands-on builds
Pet Zone Tropical FishSell the fish, plants and equipment that make it possible

That separation is deliberate. A gallery's job is to create desire — the other two are there once someone's ready to learn or to buy. Every installation here links out to both: Learn to build one goes to Hakkai's workshops, Shop the materials goes to Pet Zone.

Where the work comes from

Hakkai is San Diego's first dedicated aquatic art gallery for aquascaping, built by a team that visited galleries across Asia and Europe before opening their own — with a shared belief that recreating a piece of nature inside an aquarium is a legitimate art form, not just a hobby. The installations shown here, including the six built for the 2025 San Diego County Fair exhibition, come out of that gallery.

Visit in person

Hakkai is located inside Pet Zone Tropical Fish's Convoy District store — 4160 Convoy St, San Diego, open daily 10am–6pm — where the team runs regular workshops and demos and will build an aquascape with you on the spot. Pet Zone's original Mid-City location, 4266 University Ave, is the monster-fish and koi specialty shop: arowana, flowerhorn, large cichlids, rare plecos and koi. Full hours and directions for both are on the homepage.
