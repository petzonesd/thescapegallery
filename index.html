The idea

Not a store. Not a care guide.
A place to want one.

The Scape Gallery is the inspirational layer above two businesses that already do the practical work. Hakkai teaches the craft — workshops, materials, hands-on builds. Pet Zone Tropical Fish sells the fish, plants and equipment. This is where the finished work lives: photographed, described, and organized by style, so the idea of a living landscape can take hold before anyone reaches for a shopping cart or a class schedule.
