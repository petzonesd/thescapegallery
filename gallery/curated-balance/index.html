Nature Aquarium · UNS 90P · Designed by Logan Rando

Curated Balance

A seamless blend of structured hardscape and soft-flowing greenery, brought to life by a diverse and peaceful community of nano fish, shrimp and snails.

TankUNS 90P · 48.1 gallons
Lighting & filtrationChihiros 90B LED, hanging set · Delta 120 canister w/ lily pipe
SubstrateControsoil & Controsand, layered
HardscapeNatural Manten stone · manzanita wood
ReadIntermediate
StyleNature Aquarium →

Planting

Amazon swords, anubias, cryptocoryne and vallisneria were chosen for varied leaf texture, color tone and sustainable long-term growth — a plant list built to still look composed a year in, not just on move-in day.

Amazon SwordsAnubiasCryptocoryneVallisneria

Livestock

Ember and diamond tetras school through open water above otocinclus and a bristlenose pleco on cleanup duty; dwarf honey gourami and African dwarf frogs (Zaire platinum and standard) hold the midground, with amano shrimp, nerite and mystery snails, and yellow clams rounding out the community.
