Nature Aquarium · UNS 120U · Flagship display

Elderfall Refuge

The largest piece in the collection — an ultra-clear, rimless 120U built on 150 lbs of Hakkai stone and premium manzanita driftwood, holding a peaceful, colorful community of freshwater species at scale.

TankUNS 120U, ultra-clear rimless
LightingChihiros WRGB II Slim 120 · UN Light Screen background
Filtration2× Delta 120 canister filters w/ lily pipes
Hardscape150 lbs Hakkai stone · manzanita driftwood
ReadAdvanced
StyleNature Aquarium →

Planting

Dwarf hair grass and dwarf sag carpet the foreground; fissidens moss, bucephalandra, anubias, ferns, crypts and swords build out the mid- and background against the stone, planted into a Controsoil bed sized for a tank this large.

Dwarf Hair GrassDwarf SagFissidens MossBucephalandraAnubiasCrypts

Livestock

Angelfish and congo tetras give the piece its scale and movement, with pearl gourami and glowlight danios filling the water column. Amano and neocaridina shrimp, otocinclus catfish, bristlenose plecos and bamboo shrimp handle grazing and biofilm duty, with mystery and nerite snails on cleanup.
