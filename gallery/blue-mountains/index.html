Nature Aquarium · UNS 90L

Blue Mountains

A serene planted scape with vibrant schooling fish and peaceful inverts — dragon stone balanced against carpeting plants and red stem accents, inside a minimalist rimless tank.

TankUNS 90L rimless aquarium
Lighting & filtrationChihiros 90B LED · Delta 60 canister filter
SubstrateControsoil
HardscapeDragon stone layout

StyleNature Aquarium →

Planting

Dwarf hair grass carpets the foreground, with bucephalandra worked into the stone for texture. Ludwigia sp. Red and ammania supply the color contrast against the dragon stone's grey-blue tones, layered with a complementary plant mix for depth.

Dwarf Hair GrassBucephalandraLudwigia sp. RedAmmania

Livestock

A peaceful, densely stocked community built around schooling and grazing behavior: fire red shrimp, caridina culls, emerald eye rasbora, otocinclus, pygmy corydoras, white cloud mountain minnows, hengeli and chili rasboras, amano shrimp, and nerite & mystery snails on cleanup duty.
