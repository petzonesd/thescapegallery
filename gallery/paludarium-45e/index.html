Paludarium · UNS 45E

Compact, Lush & Full of Life

A minimalist paludarium that bridges underwater and above-water ecosystems in a small footprint — natural stone, layered vegetation and mixed fauna, proof that the paludarium style scales down without losing its sense of place.

TankUNS 45E rimless aquarium
Lighting & climateChihiros 45B LED · Zoomed fogger · UNS 45E glass lid
CirculationCompact circulation water pump
HardscapeEl Jefe stone · Controsand substrate

Planting

Pearl weed and parrot feather work below the waterline with anubias and bucephalandra on the stone; above it, japonica grass, club moss, homalomena, fittonia and assorted moss build the terrestrial bank into a small, dense jungle.

Pearl WeedAnubiasBucephalandraClub MossHomalomenaFittonia

Livestock

Vampire crabs patrol the terrestrial ledge while neocaridina and amano shrimp and chili rasboras keep to the water below — a small cast chosen to stay in scale with a 45E footprint.
